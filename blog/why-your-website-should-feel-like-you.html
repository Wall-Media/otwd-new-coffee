Why your website should feel like meeting you

#websites #brand-voice #small-business
By Petro Wall, Ramsgate

Most websites read like a form. Three bullet points, a stock photo and a button that says "Learn more".

They are not warm. They are not memorable. And they give nobody a reason to ring you instead of the three other people who showed up in the same search.

I think your website should feel like meeting you. Not the tidy version you put in the brochure. The actual version. The one who knows what your clients are wrestling with before they have even named it, because you have heard the same thing a hundred times.

It is harder to write than three bullet points and a stock photo. It means deciding what you actually think, and then saying it plainly enough that someone reading it at half ten at night, one eye on the telly, still knows you are talking to them.

But here is what happens when you do. Someone lands on your site and gets the feeling they have been looking for. Not "this company does the thing". The feeling of "yes, them". And that is worth all of it.

If your website does not sound like you yet, or if you are still running on a template somebody else wrote, I can help.

One coffee. No slides. Just a proper conversation about what your site could say.
